00 · The app, section by section

Six screens, one workout.

Ferro has five sections at the bottom and a panel for account and data. Here's what you'll find in each one.

Workout

At the gym

The right day of your program, exercise by exercise: sets, suggested load, automatic rest, records, photos and muscles for each exercise. Timer for AMRAP and EMOM, heart rate strap, today's classes and a bonus day for what you skipped.

Program

The plan

The program imported from your coach's Google Sheet, with an alert when it changes, or one you build yourself. Days, sets per week, rest times, notes, regular classes and courses.

Meals

What you eat

Food diary with calories and macros, barcode scanner, 150 common foods ready to use, water, saved meals, your nutritionist's diet plan and a weekly summary to send her.

Exercises

The library

More than 800 exercises with photos, muscles and instructions. If an exercise has the wrong photo, you fix it with one tap, and the fix helps everyone else too.

Progress

How you're doing

Session history, load trends, weight and body measurements, body composition reports. Export your sessions for Strava and Garmin.

Account and data

Everything under control

Sign in with Google or without an account, Google Health, Health Connect, Apple Health, theme and colors, backup to a file and account deletion whenever you want.

  1. 01

    The coach shares the sheet

    All you need is the link to their Google Sheet, with edit permission. No new software for them, no subscription.

  2. 02

    Ferro reads it

    Loads go back to the coach

    Every set you log ends up in the sheet, in that week's column. Weight and measurements too, in the MISURE (measurements) tab.

02 · Health Connect and Google Health

Connected to what you already track.

On Android phones Ferro uses Health Connect, the health store already on your phone: what your watch and scale record comes into Ferro, and your workouts go out to your other apps.

Health Connect is in the Android app: on Android 14 and later it's already on your phone, on earlier versions you install it for free from Google Play. Ferro asks for each permission only when you tap the feature that uses it, and you can revoke them anytime in the Health Connect settings.

Google Health, from the website and from iPhone, connects the same Google account. Google opens it to everyone only after a security review: for now it's active for a group of test accounts. On Android, use Health Connect, which has no such limit.

How to create the “Ferro Salute” shortcut
  1. Open the Shortcuts app and create a new shortcut named exactly Ferro Salute.
  2. Add “Get Dictionary from Input” with the Shortcut Input.
  3. Add “Get Dictionary Value” for the key peso, then “Repeat with Each”.
  4. Inside the repeat: take the item's v (value) and d (date and time) values and add “Log Health Sample” with type Weight, value v, unit kg and date d.
  5. Repeat steps 3 and 4 for grasso (Body Fat Percentage), kcal (Dietary Energy), proteine, carboidrati and grassi (in grams).
  6. In Ferro: Account → Apple Health → “Send to Health”. The first time, iPhone asks for permission to write to Health.
How to create the “Salute Ferro” shortcut
  1. Create a shortcut named exactly Salute Ferro.
  2. Add “Find Health Samples” with type Weight, in the last 30 days.
  3. With “Repeat with Each”, build the text {"v": Value, "d": "Date"} for each one, with the date in the format yyyy-MM-dd HH:mm, and join them with a comma.
  4. Compose the text {"peso":[ … ]}, encode it with “URL Encode” and open the address https://palestra-kohl.vercel.app/app/?salute= followed by the encoded text.
  5. Ferro opens, shows how many weigh-ins are new and asks for confirmation before saving them.

Action names may differ slightly between iOS versions. We'll publish both shortcuts ready to add with one tap.

03 · Beyond weights

Not just load × reps.

If your program includes running, swimming, timed planks or an AMRAP, Ferro reads and tracks them. No AI: it recognizes the lines the way your coach writes them.

Time and distance

“5 km in 25'”, “4×400 m”, “1000 m freestyle”, “plank 3×45"”: they become sets to log, and that's how you read them in your history.

Block timer

AMRAP counting down with “+1 round”, EMOM minute by minute, Tabata and intervals with rest periods, For Time counting up with a cap. Beeps in the last 3 seconds, screen kept on, and the result goes into the block.

Classes and courses

Pilates, spinning, yoga: add your regular weekly classes, even from a calendar file. On the right day you'll find them in Workout, with “Done” and “Add to calendar”.

Heart rate strap

Pair a Bluetooth strap and see your heart rate while you train. From Chrome on Android and desktop, and in the Android app.

Strava and Garmin

Every session exports to a TCX file with sport, duration, distance and heart rate. Upload it from strava.com or Garmin Connect.

Android app

From your watch

Runs and rides recorded by your watch come into Ferro from Health Connect, with no duplicates. And Ferro's workouts go to Health Connect with the right type: strength, running, cycling, swimming, pilates.

04 · Everything else

Everything else, done right.

Your coach changes it, you know it
If your coach edits the sheet, Ferro tells you and shows you what changed before updating the program.
Bonus day
Skipped an exercise this week? You'll find it in the bonus day, and in the sheet it goes into its own cell.
Rest that never gets lost
The timer starts on its own after every set, keeps the screen on and alerts you with sound and vibration. It stays accurate even with the phone locked.
Meals, water and barcodes
Scan the package, pick the grams, done. The data comes from Open Food Facts, an open database. Water, saved meals and training or rest days, with a summary to send your nutritionist.
Your nutritionist's diet plan
Ferro gives you a prompt to use with the service you prefer, attaching your diet plan file. Paste the answer and the day's meals appear, ready to tick off. No artificial intelligence inside the app.
Measurements and reports
Weight, waist, arms, body fat: by hand, from a connected scale or from a body composition report, the same way as the diet plan.
Records and history
Every new personal best is flagged while you're still under the bar. Your history also exports to Strava and Garmin.
Android widgets
In the Android app: today's workout, your meals and a quote to motivate you, right on your Home screen.

09 · Questions

Frequently asked questions.

How much does it cost?

Nothing. Ferro is free and has no ads. If you want to help cover the costs you can make a donation, once they open.

Does my coach need to do anything?

Just share their Google Sheet with you as “Editor”. Ferro recognizes the day rows, the week columns and the exercises. The cells your coach writes are never modified.

Does it work for running, swimming or functional training?

Yes. Timed or distance lines, holds and blocks (AMRAP, EMOM, For Time, Tabata) are logged as written in the program, with a timer for the blocks. Regular classes and courses are added separately.

What if I don't have a coach?

You can create and edit your program right in the app, with a library of more than 800 exercises with photos and muscles.

Does it work on iPhone?

Yes, from the browser: in Safari you add it to the Home Screen and it behaves like an app. Data goes to Apple Health with two Shortcuts (see iPhone and Apple Health). There's no vibration, because Safari doesn't allow it.

Health Connect or Google Health?

On Android, use Health Connect, from Ferro's Android app: it's on your phone, works for everyone and the data doesn't go through external servers. Google Health is for the website and iPhone, with the same Google account; until Google completes its review it's only available to a group of test accounts. On iPhone there's also Apple Health, with Shortcuts.

Why does Google show a warning when I connect my coach's sheet?

To read and write your coach's Google Sheet, Ferro asks Google for permission. Until Google's review is complete you may see the “Google hasn't verified this app” screen: tap “Advanced”, then “Go to Ferro”. Ferro only uses the sheet you connect.

Where does my data go?

Without an account it stays only on your phone. With a Google account it's in your private space, readable only by you. You can export everything to a file at any time. The details are in the privacy policy (in Italian).

How do I update the Android app?

When a new version comes out, Ferro tells you as soon as you open it. Tap “Update”, the phone downloads the file and you install it over the old one: program, workouts and settings stay.

Can I get a custom version?

Yes: name, colors, icon, exercises and products tailored for coaches, gyms and companies. See Custom versions or write to me from the contact section.

Is the code open?

Yes, Ferro is open source under the GPL-3.0 license: the code is on GitHub. You can check what it does with your data, report a problem or propose a change.

Does it work offline?

Yes. At the gym you can log your sets even without signal: the data syncs as soon as the connection is back.
